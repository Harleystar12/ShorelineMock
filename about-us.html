A family business since 2013

About Us

Shoreline Bathrooms was established in 2013, our aim being to bring a professional, family-run business, providing great bathroom design and products, to our customers. We have a large showroom with over 40 full room sets with many other displays on show to help you transform your bathroom into something extraordinary.

Our friendly team has over 70 years of combined experience to use to your advantage! Whether you’re dreaming of a complete makeover or a quick refresh, we have a huge selection for you to choose from.

Where We Started & Where We Are Now

From opening our doors in 2013 to growing into a close-knit team and a much-loved local showroom, Shoreline has always been about helping people create bathrooms they love.
